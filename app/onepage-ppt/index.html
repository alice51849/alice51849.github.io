OnePage PPT: AI Slide Maker

One page. One polished slide.

OnePage PPT: AI Slide Maker turns notes, photos, PDFs and spreadsheets into a single presentation-ready 16:9 page you can edit and export. It runs entirely on your iPhone and iPad, with no account to create.

◱

Built around one great slide

Instead of a deck you have to babysit, OnePage PPT builds one page: a clear headline, the supporting points that actually matter, and a layout chosen by a deterministic layout engine that keeps every element aligned at 1920 × 1080.

⌁

Bring in what you already have

Source material is imported on device:

  • Typed or pasted text
  • Photos from your library, or a new photo you take yourself
  • Printed text recognised with Apple's on-device Vision framework
  • Selected pages from a PDF
  • Structured CSV data, profiled into tables and highlights
↗

Export where the work continues

Every slide exports as an editable PowerPoint (.pptx) file, a PDF, or a 1920 × 1080 PNG. Titles, text, table rows and images stay as real objects in the .pptx, so the page keeps working after it leaves the app.

☾

On device, by design

The shipping planner runs locally. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK, and your projects are stored on your device.
