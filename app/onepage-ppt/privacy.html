OnePage PPT: AI Slide Maker

Privacy Policy

OnePage PPT is built so that your material never has to leave your device.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

✓

We do not collect your data

OnePage PPT: AI Slide Maker (com.alice51849.OnePagePPT) has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it does not build a profile of you. We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal data, and nothing in the app tracks you across other apps or websites.

☾

Your slides stay on your device

Slides are generated by an on-device planner and layout engine. The text, photos, PDF pages and CSV data you import, the slides that are produced, and your project history are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. They are not uploaded to us.

◉

Camera and photo library

The camera is accessed only when you choose to take a photo for a slide. The photo library is accessed only for the specific images you select. Those images are used to build your slide and are kept with your project on the device — they are never sent to us.

⌁

Text recognition

When you import a photo or a PDF page containing printed text, the text is recognised using Apple's Vision framework on your device. No image and no recognised text is sent to a server for this.

⚙

Preferences stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data in iOS user defaults on your device: your chosen interface language, your app settings, and the counters that track the free slide and remix allowance. This never leaves the device and contains nothing that identifies you.

◇

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (OnePage PPT Lifetime Pro) is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active on your Apple Account so it can unlock the paid features.

↗

What you export is up to you

Exported PowerPoint, PDF and PNG files are written where you choose. Once you share a file through another app, service or person, that file is governed by their terms and is outside our control.

◔

Children

OnePage PPT is a general-audience productivity app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

✉

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no account to access, correct, export or delete on our side; deleting the app removes the data held on your device. If you have any privacy question, email hourstag.app@gmail.com and we will reply.

↻

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated before the new version ships and the date above will change with it.
