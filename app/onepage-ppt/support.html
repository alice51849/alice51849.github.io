Creating your first slide

Tap the create button, add your source material, and let the app build the page. You can then edit the headline, body text, table rows and images directly on the canvas, or remix the design to see another layout of the same content.

◷

What you get before you buy

Without the Lifetime Pro unlock you can create three slides and remix each project once, so you can judge the result before paying. Premium styles and the PowerPoint and PDF exports are visible but locked.

↺

Purchases and restoring

OnePage PPT Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account — there is nothing to buy again.

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.

⌸

Exporting

Each slide exports as an editable PowerPoint (.pptx) file, a PDF, or a 1920 × 1080 PNG. The .pptx keeps text, tables and images as separate editable objects, so you can drop the page into an existing deck and keep working in PowerPoint or Keynote.

◈

Changing the language

Open Settings › Language. Choose System Default to follow your device, or pick any of the 50 supported languages. The change applies immediately, and right-to-left languages switch the layout too.

◉

Camera and photo permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo for a slide, and the photo library is read only for the images you pick yourself. If you declined a permission earlier, you can grant it again in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security.

⌫

Deleting your data

Projects live on your device. Deleting a project from the app removes it, and deleting the app removes every project, setting and exported file still held inside the app. Files you already exported to Files, Photos or another app are yours to manage there.
