Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17 · Lumi Studio

Lumi Studio ("we", "us") builds privacy-first apps for kids and families. This policy explains how our apps and our social media tooling handle data.

1. Information we do not collect

Our apps are designed to run on-device. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data such as your name, contacts, photos, or precise location for advertising. Most processing happens locally on your iPhone.

2. Information processed

If an app offers optional features (e.g. cloud sync or analytics), only the minimum data needed is processed, and never sold. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

3. Third-party platforms

We operate official accounts on platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, Threads and Pinterest to share promotional videos of our own apps. When we publish via these platforms' official APIs, we only post our own content; we do not access your personal account data.

3a. Searching public posts and replying

Our social tooling (the "Lumi Social Poster" app on Meta's platform, and our accounts on Bluesky and YouTube) searches public posts for people asking for an app recommendation or describing a problem one of our apps solves. When a post is clearly relevant, we may publish one reply from our official account that gives a practical tip, says openly that we are the developer, and links to the App Store page. We never send private messages, never like, follow or repost automatically, and never reply to the same person or thread twice.

What we keep: the post ID and link, the author's public handle, the post text, and our own reply, only so we do not reply twice and can check that our reply is still visible. We do not build profiles, sell or share this data, or use it for advertising. Search results that we do not reply to are discarded within 30 days; records of posts we replied to are kept for up to 12 months.

3b. Data deletion

To have any record about you or your post removed (including our reply to you), email hourstag.app@gmail.com with the link to your post or your handle, or simply reply to our comment asking us to remove it. We delete the record and our reply within 7 days and confirm by email. If you authorized one of our apps with your own social account, you can also remove that access at any time in that platform's settings (for Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions); we then delete any data tied to that authorization.

4. Children's privacy

Our kids' apps comply with COPPA and Apple's Kids Category rules: no third-party ads, no behavioral tracking, and no collection of personal information from children.

5. Contact

Questions? Contact us at hourstag.app@gmail.com or via alice51849.github.io.
